COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked
questions.

Learn what Cape Ranch can do and how to get started with your operation.

What is Cape Ranch?+

Cape Ranch is a modular agricultural management platform for organizing livestock, crop farming, gardening, machinery, inventory, staff, and reports.

Who is Cape Ranch designed for?+

It is designed for ranches, farms, agricultural businesses, operational teams, and organizations that want connected records and clearer processes.

Can multiple staff members use it?+

Yes. Organizations can manage staff roles and access permissions, subject to their subscription and the modules enabled for their organization.

Can I choose which areas to manage?+

Cape Ranch is modular, so the areas available to an organization depend on its subscription and configuration.

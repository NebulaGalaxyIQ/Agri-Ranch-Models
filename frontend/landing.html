COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions? Good thinking.

Get clear answers about how Cape Ranch fits into your daily operation.

Start with Cape Ranch
What is Cape Ranch?

Cape Ranch is a modular agricultural management platform. It connects ranching, crop activities, gardening, resources, machinery, staff workflows and reporting in one web application.

Who can use Cape Ranch?

Ranch operators, farm managers, agricultural organizations and staff teams can use modules and roles appropriate to their responsibilities.

Can I manage different operations together?

Yes. The platform includes several connected modules. Your organization's subscription and assigned permissions determine which tools are available.

Do staff members have their own access?

Organizations can work with staff accounts and role-based permissions. Administrators control authorized access for their teams.

How do I get started?

Choose Get started to register your organization, or Sign in if you already have an account.
